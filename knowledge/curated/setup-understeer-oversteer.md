# Setup: Understeer and Oversteer

## Purpose
This note summarizes common causes and setup levers for understeer and oversteer and suggests diagnostic checks.

## High-Level Causes
- Understeer: front tires reach their limit before the rear; common causes include low front grip, wrong aero split, high front load transfer, or incorrect camber/pressures.
- Oversteer: rear tires reach their limit before the front; common causes include low rear grip, excessive rear anti-roll stiffness, or trailing-throttle instability.

## Diagnostic Checks
- Compare left/right and inner/mid/outer temperatures for tire clues.
- Check hot pressures for growth or imbalance.
- Review entry speed and serve—high entry speed + understeer points to aero or front load issues.
- Monitor platform motion (pitch/roll) that may shift aero balance.

## Common Fixes
- For understeer: increase front grip (more front aero, softer front ARB, more front camber within tire limits, adjust pressures).
- For oversteer: increase rear grip (more rear aero, softer rear ARB, tweak diff settings, reduce rear slip).

## Practical Note
Treat these as hypotheses to be tested with single-variable runs and structured logs.
